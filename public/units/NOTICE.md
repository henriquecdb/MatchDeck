# Unit artwork

Transparent unit images and civilization mappings originate from [AoE II Companion](https://github.com/denniske/aoe2companion), revision `797538c97f3cd957bf700b23b90cdaf808e4fa80`.

Images are copied without modification from `assets/units/`. Mappings are derived from `data/src/helper/civs.ts`. Run `scripts/sync-unit-assets.ps1` to reproduce the bundled catalog.
